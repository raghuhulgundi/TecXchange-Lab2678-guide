# IBM TechXchange 2026, LAB-2678: Concert Operate incident investigation

Student guide for LAB-2678, a hands-on lab on IBM Concert Operate 5.2: topology, scope-based, temporal and
topological alert correlation, applications and business criticality, and a look-only tour of the AI incident features.

## Read the guide

- **Online (read here on GitHub):** [LAB-2678 Student Guide](guide/LAB-2678-Student-Guide.md)
- **PDF (print or download):** [LAB-2678-Student-Guide.pdf](LAB-2678-Student-Guide.pdf)

## Download your student kit

Your instructor gives you a student ID (sNN). Download **your own** kit from the [kits](kits) folder,
for example `kits/lab-s07.zip`, and unzip it in your Downloads folder. Each kit holds your topology files, your event
files and the senders for Mac or Linux (`send-events.sh`) and Windows (`send-events.cmd`). Section 2 of the guide walks
through it step by step.

**Lab environment:** this repository does not include any lab environment addresses or logins. Your instructor provides
the Concert Operate URL, your user name and password, and the webhook settings your kit asks for the first time you
send events.
